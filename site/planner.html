Faculty proposal. Not a university decision. Days and ideas are for discussion.

Block planner

Pick an idea, then pick a block. Each day has three 4-hour blocks and three rooms. The resources underneath update as you place ideas: student assistants, faculty lead time (3 WTUs per college), and rooms.

1. Ideas by college

Select an idea, then select a block. You can also drag an idea onto a block.

2. The week

Morning 8:30 a.m. to 12:30 p.m., afternoon 1:00 to 5:00 p.m., evening 5:30 to 9:30 p.m. Evening hours are a proposal; building access and staffing after 5:00 p.m. still need to be confirmed. Anchors in gold stay fixed.

3. Resources this plan uses

Assumptions: 3 WTUs is about 135 hours a semester (45 hours per WTU), or 9 hours a week over a 15-week semester. The shared student pool is 60 assistant hours a week. Monthly items count a quarter as much as weekly ones. Partner and Library hosts are not paid from a college's WTUs. Adjust the sliders in Expanded view.
