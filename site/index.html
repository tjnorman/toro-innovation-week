Toro Innovation Week

Five colleges. Five days. One Innovation Incubator. Each college hosts one day a week in the CSUDH Innovation Incubator, led by a faculty member with 3 WTU, built around innovation with an LA28 focus.

The idea in one minute

One college, one day

Each of the five colleges takes a weekday. The college sets the programming, and its students and faculty fill the room.

3 WTU for a faculty lead

Three WTU is 135 hours a semester, about 9 hours a week: one day. Each dean names the lead.

One shared student team

Student assistants are one pool of 60 hours a week, so every college day gets 12 hours of help without hiring its own staff.

Today the Incubator runs on one faculty director with 6 WTU and no staff. Current programs need about 730 faculty hours a semester; 270 are funded. This proposal spreads the work across the colleges that benefit from it.

The week

Every day has a college block, a Catapult studio run with Community Engaged Learning, and a student group. Tuesday and Thursday add a lunch speaker series.

Why now: LA28

  • The stadium complex on the CSUDH campus is set to host five Olympic and three Paralympic sports in 2028.
  • LA28's procurement plan aims 25% of its addressable spending at small businesses and 75% at the Los Angeles region.
  • LA County's Path to 2028 runs business readiness workshops that campuses and cities can co-host.
  • LA28's Made for Two-Eight licensing program for small local makers takes applications into February 2027.

An LA28 focus gives every college a reason to use the space, and gives Carson, Compton, and South Bay businesses a campus partner while contracts are still open.
